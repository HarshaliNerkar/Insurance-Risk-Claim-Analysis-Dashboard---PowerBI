# 🛡️ Insurance Risk & Claim Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Insurance Risk & Claim Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze insurance customer data, claim patterns, policy performance, and customer risk profiles. The dashboard provides actionable insights through interactive visualizations, helping insurance companies make informed business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze insurance claim trends and patterns.
- Monitor claim approval and rejection rates.
- Identify high-risk customer segments.
- Evaluate policy performance.
- Track business KPIs.
- Support data-driven decision-making.
